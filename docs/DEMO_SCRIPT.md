# Demo Script — 3 to 5 Minute Live Judge Presentation

## Act 1: The Problem & The Difference (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Landing Screen with Live Telemetry banner and Persona Switcher.
- **Narrative**: *"Every career platform asks candidates what skills they claim to have and generates generic AI advice. We built the Career Intelligence Instrument: an evidence-aware platform that asks: What career can you actually prove you are ready for?"*

## Act 2: Profile & Evidence Dossier (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Action**: Switch to **Persona B (Data Analyst)**.
- **Visual**: Show the Evidence Dossier with transcript artifacts, SQL coursework, and Python project repos.
- **Narrative**: *"Notice how the system classifies skills into Claimed, Detected, Evidenced, and Validated. Self-reported claims are never treated as verified mastery."*

## Act 3: Ready Now vs Reachable & Explainability (1:30 - 2:30)
- **Action**: Click on **Career Intelligence**.
- **Visual**: Show *Data Analyst* as **Ready Now (82%)** and *Machine Learning Engineer* as **Reachable (54%)**.
- **Action**: Open the **"Explain This"** drawer on Data Analyst.
- **Visual**: Show the mathematical breakdown (Coverage: 91%, Evidence: 78%, Blocker Penalty: -8% for missing Power BI).
- **Narrative**: *"Nothing here is a black-box LLM hallucination. Every percentage is an inspectable formula."*

## Act 4: The Prerequisite DAG & Gap Engine (2:30 - 3:15)
- **Action**: Switch to **Persona C (ML Explorer)** and open **Gap Intelligence**.
- **Visual**: Interactive DAG showing Python → Databases/APIs → Docker & Containerization → MLOps.
- **Narrative**: *"The system does not give a flat list of 20 missing tools. It computes an ordered dependency graph answering: What must I learn first?"*

## Act 5: Interactive Validation Assessment & Live Recalculation (3:15 - 4:15)
- **Action**: Click **"Launch Assessment"** on Docker & Containers.
- **Visual**: Complete a 3-question mini-quiz live and submit.
- **Visual**: Watch the score calculate (100%), the Docker skill flip from `Claimed` to `Validated`, and the ML Engineer career readiness jump live from 54% to 68%!
- **Narrative**: *"Active problem-solving instantly updates competency confidence and recalibrates role readiness across the board."*

## Act 6: Closing Summary (4:15 - 4:45)
- **Narrative**: *"From raw messy evidence to structured competency, deterministic scoring, ordered learning paths, and instant recalculation — this is the future of career intelligence."*
